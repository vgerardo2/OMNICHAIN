THE CONNECTED WORKFLOW

A routine task.
A connected attack.

The AI reads documents and uses tools to look up information or send messages. Its task is to summarize this invoice for an approved teammate. An attacker tries to turn those connected tools against it.

THE ENTRY POINT

The attack enters
through the invoice.

A hidden note tells the AI to collect private details and send them elsewhere. This prompt injection starts the chain. The attack depends on the AI turning that note into actions through its connected tools.
